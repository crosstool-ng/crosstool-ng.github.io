---
title: AI Bots Making the Web Worse
---
Like many other FLOSS projects running on the goodwill of others (thanks OSUOSL
you're awesome). Our web infrastructure is being hit relatively constantly by AI
bots.

Despite being told via robots.txt not to they seem to want to traverse the
entirety of the of the old mercurial and git repositories that we've left for
historical reference. Special shout out to "Lightpanda/1.0" which seems to be
sending GETs for URLs not even hosted by our infrastructure.

So in addition to the ban-hammer for "Lightpanda/1.0" I've had to turn off the
web interface for the old repositories. This shouldn't really affect anyone as
the current source is on https://github.com/crosstool-ng/crosstool-ng/ and has
been for some time.
